I can't hear anything

Turn the volume up. Bear & Bloom plays sound even with the silent switch on. If the speaker button at the top of the app looks faded, tap it to turn sound back on. In Grown-up settings, Play a test checks the voice.

How do I start the free trial or subscribe?

Tap any game with a padlock, or Unlock all games at the bottom of the home screen. A grown-up answers a times-table question, then you'll see the Full Access price, the free trial (for new subscribers) and the terms before anything is charged. Apple asks you to confirm.

I subscribed on another iPhone or iPad. How do I unlock this one?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Grown-up settings and tap Restore purchases. It's also on the Full Access screen.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Bear & Bloom and tap Cancel Subscription, at least 24 hours before the renewal date. In the app, Grown-up settings > Manage subscription takes you to the same place. You keep Full Access until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't see or refund App Store payments ourselves.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every game, picture and voice works without the internet. If you subscribe, the app remembers your subscription so it keeps working on car trips and planes, and checks with the App Store again next time you're online.

Which games are free?

Flashcards, Find It!, Trace It, First Sounds, Spelling Practice, Count With Me, Tell the Time, Planet Line-Up, How Do I Feel?, Calm Breathing and My Explorer Book. Full Access unlocks all 41 games.

How do I reset stars and stickers?

In Grown-up settings, tap Reset stars and stickers twice. Deleting the app also removes everything it saved on the device.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later. iPad works in every orientation and in Split View.

Privacy Policy

Bear & Bloom: Kids Learning · Effective 26 October 2026

Bear & Bloom: Kids Learning ("the app") is made by Brittany Kidd, a sole trader in Australia trading as Bear & Bloom ("we", "us"). We built the app for children, so it is designed to collect no personal information at all. This policy explains what that means in plain words.

The short version

  • We do not collect, store or share any personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Everything your child does in the app stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves a small amount of information only on your device, so it can remember where your child is up to:

  • stars, stickers and progress in activities
  • the dinosaurs, planets, animals and body parts collected in My Explorer Book
  • Star Chart and tooth chart entries
  • My Day timetable choices
  • settings such as Try first mode, Slow voice, sound and colour style
  • whether a Full Access subscription is active and when it next renews or ends, so the app can keep working offline

We never send this information to us or anyone else, and we cannot see it. You can remove it at any time by deleting the app. If your device backs up to iCloud, these settings may be included in your own device backup, which Apple handles under its privacy policy. We do not have access to it.

Read-aloud

Read-aloud uses the speech voice built into your iPhone or iPad. Words are spoken by your device. Nothing is recorded and nothing is sent over the internet. The app does not use the microphone or camera.

Subscriptions and purchases

The app is free to download, and some games are free to play. All games unlock with Bear & Bloom Full Access, an auto-renewable monthly subscription sold through the App Store (with a free trial for new subscribers where offered).

  • Apple processes all payments, renewals, cancellations and refunds. We do not receive your name, email address, payment details or Apple ID.
  • To check whether Full Access is active, the app asks Apple's StoreKit on your device. Apple tells the app only which subscription is active and when it renews or ends. This stays on your device; the app has no server and sends nothing to us.
  • You can manage or cancel the subscription at any time in your Apple ID settings (Settings > your name > Subscriptions), or from Manage subscription in the app's Grown-up settings.

Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's own privacy policy. The subscription is also covered by Apple's standard Terms of Use (EULA): https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

No third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social media or other third-party software development kits (SDKs). It does not track your child across other apps or websites. It works fully offline. The only time it uses the internet is when your device's App Store (StoreKit) checks or processes a subscription, which Apple handles.

Parental gate

Purchases, Restore purchases, Manage subscription, Grown-up settings and every link that leads out of the app (Apple's subscription page, this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use) are protected by a parental gate. This is a times-table question that a grown-up answers, so that children can't make purchases, change settings or leave the app by accident.

Children's privacy

Bear & Bloom is made for children aged 2 to 11, and we take their privacy seriously.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because we collect nothing, we have no children's information to see, change, share or delete.
  • This approach follows the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), and the UK and EU data protection rules (including the UK Age Appropriate Design Code): we only handle what is needed, and in this app that is nothing beyond what stays on your own device.
  • The app follows Apple's rules for apps in the App Store Kids Category.

If you believe personal information about a child has somehow reached us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Contacting us

If you email us for support, we will use your email address and message only to reply to you. We will not use it for marketing or share it. We will delete it when it is no longer needed to help you.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and the effective date above before the change takes effect. If a change involves collecting any information from children, we will get verifiable parental consent first, where the law requires it.
